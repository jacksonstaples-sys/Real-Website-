Average Club Distance Reference

Understanding standard club distances helps you make confident swings. Here is a breakdown of baseline ranges used by our caddie tool:

Woods & Hybrids

  • 220+ Yds Driver: Maximum distance off the tee.
  • 200-220 Yds 3-Wood: Long distance off tee or fairway.
  • 185-200 Yds 5-Wood / Hybrid: Versatile long approach club.

Irons & Wedges

  • 170-185 Yds 5-Iron: Long iron for controlled distance.
  • 145-160 Yds 7-Iron: Go-to mid-iron for green approaches.
  • 100-120 Yds Pitching Wedge (PW): High scoring club.
  • 75-95 Yds Sand Wedge (SW): Soft landing green approach.

Pro Rules for Distance Control

  • When in doubt, club up: Amateurs hit short far more often than long. Take one extra club and swing easy.
  • Account for cold weather: Cold air is denser than warm air; expect shots to travel 3 to 5 yards shorter in cold temps.
  • Trust the wind calculation: Headwinds magnify ball spin and cause shots to balloon, costing more yardage than expected!
