Pro Rules for Distance Control

  • When in doubt, club up: Amateurs hit short far more often than long. Take one extra club and swing easy.
  • Account for cold weather: Cold air is denser than warm air; expect shots to travel 3 to 5 yards shorter in cold weather.
  • Trust the wind calculation: Headwinds magnify ball spin and cause shots to balloon, costing more yardage than expected!
