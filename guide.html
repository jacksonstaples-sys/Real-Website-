Course Strategy & Environmental Playbook

Lowering your score isn't just about swinging harder—it's about smart course management. Use this strategic caddie guide to master weather variables, green reading, and essential course rules.

Environmental Factor Masterclass

Wind & Air Density

Wind affects ball flight exponentially rather than linearly. A 10 mph headwind adds more distance than a 10 mph tailwind subtracts because headwinds increase spin rate and drag.

  • Headwind Add 1 club (+10 to 12 yds) and swing easy.
  • Tailwind Subtract 1 club (-8 to 10 yds).

Green Reading & Turf

Target selection and reading grass grain will save you 3-5 strokes per round on approach shots and putts.

  • Aim Green Center Avoid short-sided pin hunting near hazards.
  • Grass Grain Dark dull grass indicates putting against the grain (slower).

Essential Rules & Relief Guide

  • Penalty Area (Red Stakes) Take lateral relief 2 club-lengths from entry point (1-stroke penalty).
  • Out of Bounds (White Stakes) Stroke and distance relief required (1-stroke penalty).
  • Casual Water Free relief within 1 club-length of nearest point of relief.
